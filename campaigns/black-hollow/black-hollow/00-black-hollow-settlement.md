## Holdings

### (Land) Black Hollow Village (18 Lands, 15 Wealth)

A heart of quiet perseverance nestled between open plains and the salt-touched breezes of the coast, Black Hollow Village stands as the cradle of civilization in these lands. Its modest thatched cottages cluster along narrow earthen lanes, the air ever tinged with woodsmoke and the distant call of gulls. Local legend claims the surrounding fields—fertile and wide—protect the settlement from harsher wilds, while the nearby coastline provides both livelihood and a hint of mystery in its tidal secrets.

At the village’s center stands the humble temple of the Dawn Saint, its weathered stones aglow each sunrise. Here, **Priest Alderic** tends to spiritual needs and community disputes alike—a gentle soul known for his patient wisdom, quietly resolute faith, and uncanny knack for knowing when a villager needs help before they can admit it themselves. Alderic is both confidante and conscience for Black Hollow, maintaining a sense of hope even as shadows lengthen at the edges of daily life. His presence is felt as much in the laughter of children beneath the eaves as in the solemn prayers for good harvests or safe returns from the sea.

- **Plains:** +5 — Rolling fields of wild grass and rare wildflowers, perfect for grazing livestock and supporting modest agriculture.
- **Coast:** +3 — Access to the brackish waters of the estuary, offering humble fishing and occasional trade with drifting travelers.
- **Community, Hamlet:** +10 — A tight-knit cluster of families known for warmth, resilience, and the enduring traditions that bind them together. The village temple—guided by Alderic—serves as a quiet beacon, drawing folk together in both hardship and celebration.

### (Holding) Black Market (Investment: 10 Wealth)

*Requirement: Law of 30 or below*

**Time to Establish:** 6 + 1d6 months

Whenever there is demand for goods forbidden by law, someone will always be daring and greedy enough to provide them. Thus, a black market flourishes—an institution usually at odds with local authorities. By establishing this holding, the House has opted to either co-opt the criminals who would otherwise fill this role, or at least skim a portion of their profits, participating in the shadows of illegality. Such an operation is only possible where the Law is relatively loose; should the king become aware, dire consequences would likely follow.

Within Black Hollow, this underground marketplace operates discreetly in the shadows of warehouses, back alleys, and shuttered cellars. Rumors swirl of deft fixers, silent deals struck beneath the watchful eyes of corrupted officials, and exotic goods changing hands in the hush before dawn.

- **Resource Gain Bonus:** Wealth +1. This bonus can be increased for a single roll by permanently sacrificing Law Resource points at a rate of 1 Law = +1 Wealth.
- **New Rules:** Illegal goods, such as poison, may be acquired with minimal fuss and at 10% less than market cost. When performing the Manage Resources House Action, the House may instantly trade Law for Wealth at a 1:1 ratio.

This black market is more than a den of crime—it is a symptom of Black Hollow's pragmatic resilience and willingness to profit where others fear to tread.


### (Personage) Priest (Investment: 5 Wealth)

*Requirement: Temple or Chapel*  
*Time to Establish: 1d6 months*

An ordained member of the clergy comes to serve as a spiritual retainer for the House, tending to its religious needs and providing guidance to its people. The priest acts not only as an intermediary to higher powers but also as a source of wisdom, ritual, and moral counsel—presiding over important ceremonies, blessing new ventures, and mediating disputes among retainers and villagers alike.

- **House Fortunes Roll Bonus:** +1 — The priest's presence bestows favor and a sense of hope, providing a steadying influence in times of uncertainty.
- **Resource Loss Mitigation:** Whenever the House would lose Influence, reduce that loss by 1 (to a minimum of 0) thanks to the priest's ability to rally the community and preserve reputation.

*Note:* Establishing this personage requires an existing Temple or Chapel within the settlement, with the appointment and arrival of the priest taking 1d6 months to complete.


### (Land) The Thicket (10 Lands)

A tangled enclave where wild grassland mingles with dense, primeval woods, The Thicket forms both a natural barrier and a haven of secrecy just beyond the village bounds. Brambles sprawl beneath ancient oaks, their shadows threaded with animal trails and the calls of unseen birds. In spring, pale bluebells bloom in shaded clearings; in autumn, the air grows heavy with the scent of loam and leaf. Locals whisper of old spirits and the occasional flicker of foxfire deep within the underbrush, marking The Thicket as a place both bountiful and haunted by the whispers of things unseen.

- **Plains:** +5 — Open glades rich with sunlight, supporting hardy flora and occasional grazing.
- **Woods, Dense:** +5 — Thick forest stretches, impenetrable to all but the surest pathfinders, home to rare herbs and creatures given to secrecy.

### (Land) Blackthorn Manor (9 Lands)

Perched atop a wind-swept rise, the crumbling silhouette of Blackthorn Manor commands a watchful gaze over both the village and its rolling surrounds. Once the opulent seat of a minor noble line, the manor now lies in disarray: walls cloaked in black ivy, ironwork gates rusted and tangled with thornbush, and shattered windows staring out like the hollow eyes of a skull. Despite its ruin, travelers and villagers alike sense the lingering weight of history and portent in its halls—a place ripe for secrets, rumors, or the ambitions of those daring enough to awaken its legacy.

- **Hill:** +7 — Elevated ground providing a vantage over land and sea; a stronghold’s bones beneath wild grass.
- **Ruin:** +3 — Collapse and decay hint at treasures lost, secrets entombed, and the promise of untamed restoration.
