## Powers

**Pinpoint Attack**  
Once per round, you can add 1d6 to the damage of a successful attack if your Dexterity is greater than your opponent’s, or if your opponent is experiencing one of the following conditions: Defenseless, Prone, Stunned, or Unconscious (see Chapter 2: Basic Rules for condition rules).

**Rogue’s Armor**  
You are at home in leather armor. You can ignore the Armor Penalty of leather armor altogether. It affects neither your Speed nor your Dexterity.

**Rogue’s Speed**  
When you wear leather armor or no armor, your Speed increases by 2.

**Vital Blow**  
When you take the Aim minor action, you can choose not to gain a +1 bonus to the attack roll. If you do this and the attack hits your opponent, their Armor Rating is halved (round down) against the damage of your attack. If you use Vital Blow with the Pierce Armor stunt, your damage becomes penetrating (thus ignoring AR altogether).

## Specializations

### **Bard**

You can move others with song and verse and can pluck out secrets alluded to by ancient hymns and epics.

**Novice**: You drive others to perform heroically. Once per encounter, you can take a major action and, with a successful TN 13 Communication (Performance) test, spin a tale or sing a song to inspire an ally capable of perceiving the effort. This may happen before or during an encounter. An inspired ally may reroll one failed test during the encounter. You may use the Crowd Appeal stunt with this ability to influence multiple allies.

### **Arcane Disciple**

You have studied some magic alongside your other pursuits.

**Novice:** You gain one Novice spell from any arcana. You also gain magic points equal to 5 + Willpower + 1d6.

**Expert:** You gain one Novice spell from any arcana in addition to the one you got with the Novice degree. You may also reduce the strain of casting in armor by 3. You also gain an additional 1d6 + Willpower magic points on top of those provided at Novice degree.


## Talents

### Contacts

You know how to turn even chance meetings with people into ongoing associations.

**Novice:** You can attempt to make a contact out of an NPC with a successful Communication (Persuasion) test. The GM will set the target number based on the likelihood of you knowing the NPC or having mutual friends. The more distant the NPC’s homeland or social class from yours, the more difficult the test will be. A contact will be friendly, but won’t go out of their way to help you without additional motivation. You can’t make a contact out of an NPC who already dislikes you or is an enemy.


### Thievery

What's yours is yours—and what's theirs might soon be yours as well.

**Novice (_Dexterity (Lock Picking)_):** Select one focus from Dexterity (Lock Picking), Dexterity (Traps), Perception (Searching), or Intelligence (Thieves’ Lore). If you fail a test with this focus, you may reroll it, but must accept the result of the second roll.

### Performance

You have prodigious talents as an actor, musician, or other performing artist.

**Novice:**  
You are skilled in the arts of entertainment — able to play an instrument, sing, act, and otherwise captivate an audience. When using the Communication (Performance) focus, the Crowd Appeal stunt costs only 1 SP per person for you.

**Expert:**  
With a keen sense for atmosphere, you can read a room and adjust your performance accordingly. If you spend at least 15 minutes observing a crowd before performing or speaking to them, you gain a +1 bonus on all Communication-based tests made to influence or entertain that audience.

## Spells

### Disorient

**Requirements:** Illusion Arcana (Novice)  
**Spell Type:** Attack  
**MP Cost:** 4  
**Casting Time:** Major Action  
**Target Number:** 11  
**Test:** Willpower (Self-Discipline) vs. Spellpower

You distort the senses of one visible character within 10 yards. The target makes a Willpower (Self-Discipline) test against your Spellpower. On a failure, the target suffers the Slowed condition (see Chapter 2: Basic Rules) and takes a –2 penalty to tests other than those affected by being Slowed. At the start of each of your turns, the target may attempt another Willpower (Self-Discipline) test; a success ends the spell. Otherwise, the effect lasts for the remainder of the encounter.

### Figment

**Requirements:** Illusion Arcana (Novice)  
**Spell Type:** Utility  
**MP Cost:** 2  
**Casting Time:** Minor Action  
**Target Number:** 10  
**Test:** Perception (Seeing) vs. Spellpower

You conjure a single simple visual illusion. The figment can look like anything you’ve seen or can describe (at the GM’s discretion), but can be no larger than 2 cubic yards in size. It has no substance and makes no sound. Anyone with a reason to believe the figment might not be real can roll a Perception (Seeing) test against your Spellpower to see through it. You can impart motion to the illusion by taking a minor action each round to manipulate it, and the figment lasts for a minute; spend an additional 2 MP to extend its duration for another minute when it expires.
